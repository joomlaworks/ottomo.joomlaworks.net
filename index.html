The mail robot for your Joomla site

Ottomo designs, sends and tracks email newsletters from your Joomla administrator. Your subscribers stay in your own database, your emails leave through your server or the mail service you choose, and every open and click is counted on your site. Free and open source.

Install in three steps

One package installs everything: the component, the signup module, the control panel module, the mail service plugins, the content plugins and the command-line commands.

  1. Download

    Get the Ottomo 1.1 ZIP, or from the release on GitHub.

  2. Upload

    In Joomla's administrator, go to System > Install > Extensions (Extensions > Install on Joomla 3) and upload the ZIP as it is.

  3. Set up

    In Components > Ottomo > Settings, set your sender and mailer and press Test connection. Add a topic, put the signup module on your site, and write your first campaign.

Requirements: Joomla 3.x UTD, Joomla 4, 5 or 6 (tested on 3.18, 4.4, 5.4 and 6.2); PHP 7.1 to 8.5, within what your Joomla supports; MySQL or MariaDB, PostgreSQL, or SQLite on Joomla 3.x UTD. Updates arrive through Joomla's own update manager.

From idea to inbox in four steps

Every campaign follows the same wizard: who gets it, how it looks, what it says, and sending it. Finished steps get a tick, and you can go back to any of them.

  1. Details and recipients. Subject, preview text and sender, then tick the topics to send to. Ottomo counts the active subscribers it will reach, each person once.
  2. Design. Pick one of the six email templates, or one of your own.
  3. Content. Write in the designer, with articles, images and HTML blocks, and personal touches like {{firstname|there}}.
  4. Send. A pre-flight checklist, a test email, then batches with a progress bar. If the tab closes, the campaign resumes with the people still waiting, and nobody gets it twice.

Afterwards, the same page shows the campaign's recipients, unique open and click rates and its most clicked links. Duplicate any campaign as a new draft to send an improved version.

A designer that writes like a document

Click into the email and type. No blocks to configure, no HTML to learn, and the result is email-safe HTML that renders in Gmail, Apple Mail and Outlook.

Edit in place

Bold, italic, headings, lists, alignment, links and an email-safe font menu, right on the email. Pasted text arrives clean.

Add content

A "+" in every position adds Joomla articles, K2 items, images from the Media Manager, buttons or your own HTML. Images fit their column; a click on an image or a button opens its settings.

Drag and drop

Move blocks within and between positions with the mouse, or with the arrow keys.

HTML and plain text

A code editor for full control, kept in sync with the visual editor, and a plain-text version sent alongside.

Desktop and phone

Switch the preview width while you write; columns stack on phones on their own.

Personal

First name, company, city and other profile fields, in the subject and the content, with a fallback when a value is missing.

Six email templates, light and dark

600 pixels wide, responsive, with sample content to start from. Each has its own dark palette for Apple Mail, iOS Mail and other dark-mode clients, and renders properly in classic Outlook for Windows.

  • Minimal, 1 columnClean everyday newsletter
  • Minimal, 2 columnsA main column and a sidebar
  • AnnouncementOne message, one call to action
  • StudioEditorial, with a serif headline and a dark closing band
  • LinenWarm neutrals, product, quote and event cards
  • Template StarterPlain and documented: the base for your own

Make your own: drop a folder into your site template's html/ottomo/ and Ottomo picks it up, or install a template package. The template guide documents the format, every placeholder and the email HTML that works everywhere.

Made to reach the inbox

Gmail, Yahoo and Outlook have strict rules for bulk senders. Ottomo follows them by default, and checks your setup before you press Send.

  • One-click unsubscribe headers (RFC 8058), as Gmail and Yahoo require of bulk senders.
  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC of your sender's domain checked in the pre-flight checklist and in Settings.
  • No Gmail clipping: emails are minified, and the checklist shows the size against Gmail's 102 KB limit.
  • Footers Gmail won't fold away, and a Feedback-ID header for Gmail Postmaster Tools.
  • Double opt-in for new subscribers, and unsubscribed addresses kept as suppressed: never re-added by an import.
  • A compliance footer with your postal address, and your own line on why people get the email.

Send now

Send through your server, or a mail service

PHP mail, Sendmail and SMTP work out of the box. For large lists, connect one of six mail services with an API key: each is a small plugin, with no SDK to install.

  • Amazon SESPaced to your account's send rate
  • BrevoPer-recipient personalisation
  • MailgunUS and EU regions
  • MailjetPer-message results
  • SendGridWeb API v3
  • SendPulseThrottled emails retried
  • SMTPOne connection per batch
  • PHP mail, SendmailJoomla's own mailer

Mail services report their own figures to the dashboard. Whatever you send through, batches go only to subscribers who haven't received the campaign, so an interrupted send resumes where it stopped.

Your audience, on your site

Subscribers live in your Joomla database, not in someone else's cloud. Bring in the people you already have in a few clicks.

Import from what you already run

Each source shows how many addresses it holds, and imports in batches that keep any site within PHP's time limits: 50,000 forum members take about four minutes.

  • Joomla Users: activated accounts that aren't blocked
  • AcyMailing: subscribers, and those who left as suppressed
  • Kunena and Meela: forum members who can sign in
  • VirtueMart and HikaShop: customers and guest buyers, with their addresses
  • CSV or pasted text, and a CSV export of what you see

Topics are your mailing lists. Subscribers can be in several, with a profile (company, city, phone and more) and a status: subscribed, pending or unsubscribed. Private topics stay off the site: lists you manage yourself, like test recipients.

Opens and clicks, without third parties

A tracking pixel counts opens and every link counts clicks, stored on your site. External links are signed, so the tracker can't be used as an open redirect.

  • The dashboard: sent, opened and clicked over a day, week, month or any range, per topic
  • Every campaign: recipients, unique open and click rates and its most clicked links
  • The control panel module: the same statistics on Joomla's own dashboard, with quick links
  • Mail services add their own delivery figures

Signup forms that look like your site

The signup module comes in two layouts, a compact one for sidebars and a full-width section with topics as chips. Its CSS steps aside, so your template's fonts, fields and buttons apply.

  • Choose the fields: the topics offered, the profile fields shown, which are required, and an intro text
  • Spam protection with Joomla's captcha plugins and a honeypot field
  • Double opt-in with an activation email in HTML and plain text
  • A preferences page linked from every email: profile, topics, unsubscribe or delete the account
  • A public archive of sent campaigns, each readable in the browser

One package, Joomla 3.x UTD to Joomla 6

The same ZIP installs on Joomla 3.x UTD, 4, 5 and 6, and Ottomo looks the same in Prothero and Atum, in light and dark, on a wide screen, a tablet or a phone.

At home in every administrator

Ottomo keeps its own sidebar in every Joomla. When the room gets tight, under Atum's menu or on a tablet, it folds into a menu button that slides the sidebar over the page.

Plain code you can extend

Plain PHP, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, with no build step and no jQuery. Templates, content sources, mail services and import sources are all small, documented pieces.

Command line

List, send or resume campaigns from cron, import subscribers, purge unsubscribed addresses and check the sending settings. JSON output and dry runs included.

Email templates

A folder with a manifest, a PHP layout and sample content; overridable from your site template, installable as a package.

Plugin events

Add content sources to the designer's "+" menu, or a mail service with onOttomoSend(), onOttomoVerify() and onOttomoStatistics().

Secure by default

CSRF tokens on every action, one permission check for every route, escaped output, signed click tracking and hashed subscriber links.

What's new

Every release, in short. Each change is described in the full changelog on GitHub.

v1.1November 24, 2026
latest

Buttons and image settings in the designer, private topics

The first update: more control over text and images in the designer, mailing lists that stay off the site, and fixes.

  • Buttons: add one from the "+" menu in your template's style, type its text, and set its link, alignment, width, colours and corners. They work in classic Outlook too.
  • Text alignment (left, center, right) in the designer's toolbar.
  • Image settings: click an image to set its width, alignment, alt text and link. Images fit their column automatically, also in classic Outlook, so a 1920px photo no longer widens the email.
  • Private topics: hidden on the site (signup forms, preferences, archive) but usable for campaigns, e.g. for test recipients.
  • Save & New for topics and subscribers, and test emails to several addresses at once, comma separated.
  • Ottomo's version and release date at the foot of its menu.
  • Fixed: a test email was sent twice; selecting a link in the designer made it jump; copied icons and images lost everything but their text; saving subscription preferences removed hidden topics.
